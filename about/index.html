About Al Qaser Al Shamali

Al Qaser Al Shamali is a UAE interior decoration business focused on gypsum ceilings, integrated LED lighting, painting and modern-classic finishes. With more than 17 years of experience and over 750 completed projects, the team serves villa owners, contractors and developers across its service areas.

Interior finishing built around measured site work

The team serves villa owners, contractors and developers in Al Ain, Abu Dhabi and Dubai. Projects begin with a site review, a written scope and coordination between ceiling design, lighting and finishes.

Clear scope

Rooms, materials, exclusions and payment stages are written before mobilisation.

Coordinated trades

Gypsum, lighting, AC openings, curtains and paint are reviewed together.

Sample approval

Colour, sheen, profile size and light temperature are approved where practical.

Handover checks

Alignment, joints, lighting and snag items are checked before closure.
