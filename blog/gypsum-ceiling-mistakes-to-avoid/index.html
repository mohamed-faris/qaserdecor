10 gypsum ceiling mistakes to avoid

Most disappointing ceilings are not caused by one dramatic error. They come from small decisions that were never coordinated.

Published 6 October 20266 minute read
Editorial note

This is general planning guidance. The final specification depends on site measurement, project requirements and the approved system.

Design mistakes

Avoid copying a ceiling from a room with a different height and proportion. Too many levels, curves or light colours can lower the visual ceiling and fragment the space.

Do not distribute spotlights before furniture and ceiling axes are known. Random rows remain visible even when the fixtures are off.

Coordination mistakes

Do not close the frame before AC grilles, curtains, sprinklers where applicable, chandeliers and access points are located. Late openings weaken the finish and disrupt symmetry.

Never seal LED drivers or service valves inside the ceiling. Maintenance access is a design requirement, not an afterthought.

Structural and fixing mistakes

Heavy fixtures need independent, suitable support. They should not hang from the face board. Framing centres and hangers must follow the selected system and the actual loads.

Do not use a decorative ceiling to hide unresolved cracks, movement or water leaks in the structure above. Diagnose and repair the source first.

Finishing mistakes

Rushing joint coats, sanding before material dries or painting without suitable primer can leave bands and fastener marks. Grazing cove light makes these flaws more obvious.

External corners and repeated reveals should be checked with a straight edge. A small deviation multiplied around a room becomes a visible pattern.

Handover mistakes

Do not inspect only under bright daytime work lights. Test every final lighting scene, including dimmed coves and wall washers, and view from the entrance and seating positions.

Record access-panel locations, driver positions and any approved material information. Good handover protects the completed ceiling long after photographs are taken.
