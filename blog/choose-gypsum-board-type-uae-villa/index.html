Choosing gypsum board for a UAE villa

Board colour is not a complete specification. The room condition, framing and full tested assembly matter.

Published 6 October 20266 minute read
Editorial note

This is general planning guidance. The final specification depends on site measurement, project requirements and the approved system.

Begin with the room condition

A dry bedroom ceiling does not face the same exposure as a selected humid utility area. Identify moisture sources, ventilation, leaks and service access before selecting the board.

No interior gypsum board should be used to conceal an unresolved water problem. Repair the source and allow the structure to dry before closing the area.

Regular and moisture-resistant boards

Regular board is commonly used in suitable dry internal locations. Moisture-resistant board can be specified for selected humid interiors, but it is not waterproofing and does not replace ventilation.

Follow the board manufacturer's system guidance and the consultant's approved specification where a project has formal requirements.

Fire performance is a complete system

A fire-rated result depends on the tested combination of board layers, framing, fixings, insulation, joints and penetrations. Choosing a board by colour alone does not establish a fire rating.

Openings for lights, access panels and services must be detailed so the required assembly remains valid. Seek the approved system information for regulated areas.

Framing and fixing matter just as much

Even the correct board can perform poorly on inadequate or widely spaced framing. Ceiling loads, profile spacing, hangers and perimeter details need to follow the appropriate system.

Decorative centres, chandeliers and heavy wall items require separate support as specified. They should never rely on a face board without suitable backing.

Ask for a written material description

A quotation should name the board type or intended performance, thickness where relevant, number of layers, framing approach and included finish.

Keep product labels or delivery records for the project. Clear documentation makes inspection easier and avoids disagreements once the boards have been painted.
