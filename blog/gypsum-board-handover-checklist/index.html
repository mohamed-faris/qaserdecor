A gypsum-board handover checklist

Inspect geometry, surfaces, lighting and access as one finished system—then record anything that needs correction.

Published 6 October 20266 minute read
Editorial note

This is general planning guidance. The final specification depends on site measurement, project requirements and the approved system.

Check lines and proportions

Stand at the main entrance and view the entire ceiling or wall. Confirm that centres, borders and repeated panels align with the approved layout.

Use a straight edge where needed. Look for waviness, uneven margins, inconsistent shadow gaps and corners that change width.

Inspect surfaces under useful light

Daylight, downlights and concealed coves reveal different conditions. Switch through every circuit and inspect from more than one angle.

Look for joint bands, fastener marks, sanding scratches, paint variation and cracks. Distinguish a surface issue from a shadow created by the intended geometry.

Test openings and coordinated items

Confirm spotlight and linear-light openings are straight and clean. Check chandelier centres and the meeting points around grilles, curtains and wall details.

Open every access panel and make sure it can be removed without damaging the paint. Verify that the item it serves is actually reachable.

Review protection and cleanup

Check adjacent floors, walls, windows and fittings for dust, paint or impact damage. Agree how any protected areas will be cleaned and restored.

Remove labels and construction debris, but keep useful records of board or system information where a formal specification applies.

Write a clear snag list

Photograph each issue, identify the room and describe the expected correction. Group related items so they can be resolved efficiently.

After corrections, repeat the relevant lighting and access tests. Final acceptance should follow the agreed scope, not only a quick visual impression.
