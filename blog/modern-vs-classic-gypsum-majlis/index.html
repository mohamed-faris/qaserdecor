Modern or classic gypsum for an Abu Dhabi majlis?

The right answer is less about trend and more about architecture, room height and the atmosphere your majlis should carry.

Published 6 October 20266 minute read
Editorial note

This is general planning guidance. The final specification depends on site measurement, project requirements and the approved system.

Read the architecture first

A majlis with tall arched windows, formal symmetry and a central chandelier can support classic order. A lower open-plan room with broad glazing often benefits from a quieter modern ceiling.

The gypsum style should connect to the building's openings and proportions. Adding classical ornament to an unrelated shell may feel applied rather than integrated.

What makes a modern ceiling work

Modern does not mean placing random LED lines across a white surface. Strong modern work has a clear axis, controlled recesses and disciplined spacing.

Use a small family of details: perhaps a perimeter shadow, a central plain plane and aligned lighting. The quality comes from straight lines and consistent reveals.

What makes a classic ceiling work

Classic work relies on hierarchy. A main centre, supporting borders and selected ornament should not all compete at the same strength.

Keep relief depth proportional to the ceiling height. Decorative panels need enough surrounding space, and repeated motifs should align with wall bays or seating zones.

The transitional option

A transitional majlis can keep classical symmetry while reducing ornament. A simple moulding, broad tray and central chandelier may provide enough formality.

This approach is useful when the architecture is traditional but the furniture and lighting are more contemporary. It also tends to age more quietly than highly layered motifs.

Make the decision with a marked layout

Review a reflected-ceiling drawing and mark the proposed drops on the wall. If possible, outline one important corner or panel at full scale.

Compare the design with curtain height, AC grilles and chandelier size. The chosen style is successful when every element has room to be seen.
