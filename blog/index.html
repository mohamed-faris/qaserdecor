Know before you build.

Eleven practical guides in English and Arabic, written to help villa owners and project teams make clearer gypsum-board decisions.

01Planning

A practical guide to gypsum ceiling design in Abu Dhabi

The best ceiling is not the one with the most details. It is the one whose levels, light and proportions belong to the room.

+02Walls

Gypsum wall decoration and TV feature walls

A wall detail succeeds when it organises the elevation and hides the practical work without becoming visually noisy.

+03Style

Modern or classic gypsum for an Abu Dhabi majlis?

The right answer is less about trend and more about architecture, room height and the atmosphere your majlis should carry.

+04Materials

Choosing gypsum board for a UAE villa

Board colour is not a complete specification. The room condition, framing and full tested assembly matter.

+05Lighting

How to plan lighting with a gypsum ceiling

Lighting should shape the ceiling from the first drawing, not arrive as a collection of holes at the end.

+06Budget

What affects gypsum ceiling cost in Abu Dhabi?

A square-metre rate is only a starting point. Geometry, coordination and the condition of the site often decide the real scope.

+07Execution

Gypsum-board execution: process and timeline

A reliable programme follows the work that must be inspected, dried and approved—not just the date the room should look finished.

+08Details

Designing gypsum niches, arches and wall panels

Curves and recesses feel crafted when their dimensions follow the wall; they feel decorative when they are merely added to it.

+09Quality

10 gypsum ceiling mistakes to avoid

Most disappointing ceilings are not caused by one dramatic error. They come from small decisions that were never coordinated.

+10Handover

A gypsum-board handover checklist

Inspect geometry, surfaces, lighting and access as one finished system—then record anything that needs correction.

+11Quotation

How to prepare for a gypsum site visit and quotation

A useful first visit is not a sales performance. It is a short technical conversation that turns your idea into a measurable scope.
