Specialist scope

Gypsum-board ceiling and wall decoration and execution. We do not provide general interior decoration, furniture or joinery.

Gypsum-board work planned for the actual Abu Dhabi room

A successful ceiling begins with the room’s clear height, beams, curtain pockets, air-conditioning points and intended lighting—not with a copied online image. We review those constraints before agreeing drops, coves, shadow gaps or wall composition.

For a villa reception, majlis or private room, the setting-out is adjusted around entrances, windows and the main furniture axis. This gives the finished ceiling or wall a deliberate proportion from the positions where it will be seen.

Specialist ceiling and wall scope

Our work is limited to gypsum-board decoration and execution for ceilings and walls. The scope can include the details that make a modern gypsum composition work as one system.

  • False ceilings, recessed trays, coves and shadow gaps
  • Curtain pockets, spotlight openings and linear-light channels
  • Gypsum-board TV walls, niches, arches and framed wall panels
  • Metal framing, boards, jointing and finish preparation
  • Coordination of access openings with the responsible services trades

A clear route from site survey to handover

Share the Abu Dhabi project area, room type, current condition and a few useful references. We use the survey to confirm levels, openings and any conditions that affect the buildable scope.

Before boards are closed, framing geometry and service locations should be checked. At handover, surfaces, lighting effects and maintenance access are reviewed as one finished composition.
