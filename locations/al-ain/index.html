Gypsum board work in Al Ain

Based in Al Ain, Qasr Alshamali specialises in the measured design detailing and site execution of gypsum-board ceilings and walls for villas, majlis rooms and selected projects.

Request a site visit
Specialist scope

Gypsum-board ceiling and wall decoration and execution. We do not provide general interior decoration, furniture or joinery.

Start with a measured Al Ain site survey

The ceiling level that suits one room may not suit another. We begin by reading the existing dimensions, beams, windows, curtain treatment, air-conditioning points and intended lighting before the gypsum layout is confirmed.

That information guides the board levels, centre lines, openings and maintenance access. It also prevents a reference image from being forced into a room where its proportions do not work.

Modern gypsum-board details for ceilings and walls

A restrained modern detail is built from accurate framing and controlled transitions. We focus on the gypsum-board parts of the composition and coordinate related service locations before closure.

  • Recessed and multi-level gypsum-board ceilings
  • Concealed-light coves, curtain pockets and shadow-line details
  • Television-wall backgrounds, niches, framed panels and arches
  • Openings for downlights, linear profiles, chandeliers and access panels
  • Jointing and surface preparation for the agreed finishing system

What makes execution dependable

The visible finish relies on decisions that disappear behind the boards: framing alignment, backing, service coordination and time for joint treatment. Those items are reviewed before the next stage covers them.
