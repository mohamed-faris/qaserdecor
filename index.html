FAQ

Frequently asked questions about gypsum and interior finishing

Which areas do you serve?

We accept enquiries and site visits in Al Ain, Abu Dhabi and Dubai, subject to project size and schedule.

How much does a gypsum ceiling cost in Abu Dhabi?

Pricing follows site measurement and depends on area, board type, framing, levels, curves, access panels, lighting details and site access.

Can gypsum ceilings include LED cove lighting?

Yes. The cove, strip, drivers, spotlights and maintenance access should be coordinated before the gypsum ceiling is closed.

How long does a typical room take?

A straightforward single-room gypsum ceiling often takes about 3–7 working days after design and material approval.

Which gypsum boards can be used?

Regular, moisture-resistant and fire-rated boards can be specified according to the room and approved project requirements.

Do you inspect the site before quoting?

Yes. Measurement confirms dimensions, existing conditions, electrical points, access and the final scope.

Is there a warranty?

Warranty coverage depends on the agreed materials and scope and should be written into the final quotation or contract.
