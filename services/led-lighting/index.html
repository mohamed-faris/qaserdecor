LED ceiling lighting

LED Ceiling Design & Cove Lighting Installation in Dubai

LED ceiling lighting uses concealed strips, linear profiles and recessed fixtures within a gypsum design. A reliable system starts with the layout, colour temperature, power, driver access and control method—not the strip alone.

Request a site visitWhatsApp

What is LED cove lighting?

An LED strip is hidden in a recessed ledge so light reflects from the ceiling or wall. Cove depth, distance and finish colour affect whether the light appears continuous or shows dots and shadows.

How much does LED ceiling design cost in Dubai?

Cost depends on linear metres, strip and profile specification, drivers, dimming, controls, wiring distance, access and whether new gypsum work is required. Products and labour should be listed separately.

How long does installation take?

For a prepared single-room ceiling, fitting strips, profiles, drivers and testing often takes about 1–3 working days. New gypsum, rewiring or smart controls take longer.

Which colour temperature should be used?

2700K appears very warm, 3000K is warm white and commonly suits living rooms, while 4000K is more neutral for kitchens and work areas. Test the colour against actual finishes.

How should drivers and maintenance be planned?

Drivers should be ventilated and accessible, matched to the strip and controls, and divided appropriately on long runs. Test a sample before completing the whole cove.

Can an existing ceiling be upgraded?

Sometimes. A site visit confirms cove depth, wiring route, power, driver access and ceiling condition before deciding whether gypsum changes are needed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about led ceiling lighting

Is cove lighting enough for a living room?

It is usually an ambient layer; task or accent lighting may also be needed.

Can LED cove lights be dimmed?

Yes when the strip, driver and dimmer are compatible.

Why do LED dots appear?

Shallow coves, low strip density or insufficient diffusion can make individual points visible.

Where should drivers be placed?

In a ventilated, accessible location for inspection and replacement.

Can lighting be coordinated with a new gypsum ceiling?

Yes, and early coordination improves alignment and maintenance access.
