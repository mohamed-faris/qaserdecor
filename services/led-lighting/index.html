Lighting integration

Lighting details within gypsum ceilings

We do not sell lighting as a separate décor service. We shape the gypsum-board ceiling around the approved lighting concept so coves, openings and access points are intentional.

Request a site visit
Specialist scope

Gypsum-board ceiling and wall design and execution only, with coordination of related details.

The ceiling and lighting are one composition

A cove changes the perceived height and proportion of a room. Spotlights create a rhythm across the ceiling. Linear profiles establish direction. These details should be drawn together rather than added after the gypsum is finished.

We coordinate clearances with the electrician or lighting supplier and translate the selected locations into accurate framing and cut-outs.

Typical gypsum-lighting details

The gypsum portion may include concealed-light coves, recessed channels, border details, chandelier centres and curtain-light pockets.

  • Upward or inward-facing LED coves
  • Openings for recessed spotlights
  • Channels prepared for approved linear profiles
  • Centred support zones around decorative fixtures
  • Accessible positions for drivers and connections

Maintenance is part of the detail

LED strips and drivers eventually need inspection or replacement. A sealed driver above a finished ceiling creates avoidable damage later. Access must be planned before closure.

The final colour temperature, dimming and electrical load belong to the electrical specification. Our responsibility is to keep the gypsum geometry consistent and the maintainable components reachable.

Test before the final coat

Lighting should be temporarily tested before the final paint and again at handover. This can reveal dark breaks, visible strip dots, misaligned openings or light grazing across an unfinished joint.

Small corrections at that point protect the finished appearance of both the lighting and the gypsum surface.
