Style & execution

Modern & classic gypsum-board styles

The style may be minimal, classic or somewhere between. Our task is to refine the reference, make it proportionate to the room and execute the gypsum-board details cleanly.

Request a site visit
Specialist scope

Gypsum-board ceiling and wall design and execution only, with coordination of related details.

Modern is about restraint

Modern gypsum decoration relies on alignment, shadow, clean levels and fewer intentional lines. It is not simply an empty ceiling: the proportions of a border or recess still need care.

For lower rooms, a shallow perimeter, curtain pocket or selective shadow line can give definition without making the entire ceiling feel heavy.

Classic requires proportion

Classic details work when borders, centres and wall panels share a clear scale. Copying every feature from a much larger majlis can make a normal room feel crowded.

We simplify repeated ornament where needed and keep sufficient blank space around chandeliers, windows and openings so the composition can breathe.

Transitional styles connect both

Many Abu Dhabi homes need a balance: the order and elegance of classic work with cleaner modern lines. A restrained profile around a plain tray ceiling or simple framed walls can achieve that balance.

The best result comes from choosing a few related details and repeating them consistently instead of mixing many unrelated shapes.

Execution decides whether the style works

Whatever the style, centre lines, margins, curves and repeated modules must be measured. The drawing should identify the build-up and lighting before the framing begins.

We inspect the completed geometry before jointing, then check corners and surfaces again before paint. Style is visible; disciplined execution is what makes it convincing.
