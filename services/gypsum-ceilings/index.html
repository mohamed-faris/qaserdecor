Gypsum ceilings

Gypsum Ceiling Contractor in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain & Dubai

A gypsum ceiling is a lightweight suspended ceiling made from board fixed to a metal frame, then jointed and prepared for paint. It conceals services and creates clean cove, spotlight and decorative details for UAE villas.

What is a gypsum ceiling and why choose one in the UAE?

Gypsum board creates a level surface below the slab and can conceal wiring, LED drivers and selected services. It supports trays, curves, bulkheads, shadow gaps, access panels and decorative details.

How much does a gypsum ceiling cost in Abu Dhabi?

A responsible quotation needs measurements. Price changes with area, board type, framing, number of levels, curves, access panels, lighting openings, demolition, paint and site access.

What is the difference between gypsum board and POP ceilings?

Gypsum board is a dry system fixed to metal framing and is well suited to clean planes and integrated lighting. POP is wet plaster formed on site and is often used for moulded ornament. The approved design and room conditions determine the right system.

How long does installation take?

A straightforward single-room ceiling often takes about 3–7 working days after design and material approval. Curves, multiple levels, electrical changes and drying between joint coats extend the programme.

Which board types can be specified?

Regular board may suit dry internal rooms; moisture-resistant board may be used in selected humid spaces; fire-rated work must follow the complete approved assembly, not board colour alone.

How is gypsum coordinated with LED lighting and AC?

Coves, spotlights, chandeliers, drivers, diffusers, curtains and access panels should be shown on one ceiling layout before boards are closed. Drivers and maintainable services must remain accessible.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about gypsum ceilings

Do you quote per square metre?

Area is one input, but levels, curves, board type, openings, access and paint also affect the price.

Can work be done in an occupied villa?

Yes, subject to access, protection, dust control and agreed working zones.

Can moisture-resistant board be used?

Yes where appropriate, but it does not replace waterproofing or ventilation.

Can LED lighting be included?

Yes, when positions, wiring, drivers and access are coordinated before closure.

What is needed for a quotation?

Location, dimensions or drawings, reference images, current ceiling condition, lighting requirements and preferred timing.
