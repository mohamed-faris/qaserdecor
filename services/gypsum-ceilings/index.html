Ceiling decoration

Gypsum ceiling design & execution

From a quiet shadow gap to a detailed majlis ceiling, we translate the chosen style into a buildable gypsum-board layout, coordinate the lighting positions and execute the work on site.

Request a site visit
Specialist scope

Gypsum-board ceiling and wall design and execution only, with coordination of related details.

A ceiling designed for the actual room

Good ceiling decoration begins with the room, not a catalogue image. We measure the clear height, wall geometry, curtain pockets, air-conditioning points, chandeliers and access requirements before confirming the levels.

The design is then adjusted so its centre lines, borders and drops make sense from the entrance and around the furniture layout. This avoids awkward cuts, crowded spotlights and decorative features that feel too large for the space.

What we execute

Our scope is focused on gypsum-board framing, boards, joints, decorative profiles and the details that belong to the ceiling composition.

Lighting fixtures and electrical work can be coordinated with the relevant trades, while the gypsum layout keeps coves, openings and maintenance access in the right places.

  • Flat and multi-level false ceilings
  • Coves, trays, shadow gaps and curtain pockets
  • Classic borders, centres and moulded details
  • Openings for spotlights, linear profiles and chandeliers
  • Access panels for maintainable services

Before the boards are closed

We confirm the approved ceiling drawing, drop heights, board specification, service openings and the locations of LED drivers or access panels. Changes are much easier to make while the framing is visible.

For occupied villas, the working area, protection, dust control and daily cleanup should also be agreed before execution starts.

A finish that is judged in daylight

Jointing and sanding need enough time between coats. Rushing this stage can leave visible lines under grazing light. We inspect edges, corners and transitions before the final paint system is completed.

At handover, the ceiling is checked with the lighting on and off. The goal is not only a clean photograph; it is a ceiling that remains visually balanced during everyday use.

Before requesting a price

Send the room type, project area, current photos and any useful reference. Final pricing follows measurement and an agreed detail.
